# Panna Cage

**Play:** https://muhammadalims.github.io/panna-cage/

1v1 street football (panna) in a clay 3D cage, built with Three.js in a single HTML file.

## Grounds
- **London Estate**: green steel cage between red-brick blocks. Walls are in play; ball over the cage restarts from the corner for the other player; panna and collect: +1.
- **Rio Rooftop**: rooftop pitch above the favela, low wall, no cage. Ball off the roof: the other player's ball ("go fetch it"); a caneta (panna) counts double, +2.
- **Paris Cité**: blue cage between concrete towers. A petit pont (panna) wins the round: +1 and restart.

First to 5 or a 2-minute clock; a tie goes to one minute of golden goal, then a draw.

## Modes
1v1 against the AI, or 2 players on one keyboard.

## Controls
- **1v1:** WASD or arrows to move, hold Space to power a shot (tap to tackle without the ball), E to panna, Shift to sprint.
- **2 players:** P1 WASD / Space / E / Left Shift; P2 arrows / Enter / `/` / Right Shift.
- **Phone (landscape):** left stick, hold SHOOT (TACKLE without the ball), PANNA, SPRINT.
- T cycles time of day, M mutes, P toggles the pixel look, Esc pauses, R returns to the menu.

## Development
Edit `game.html`, then run `./build.sh` to regenerate `index.html`.
